Delete your Tweak account

You can permanently delete your Tweak account and the data tied to it from here — you don't need the app installed. Requests are usually processed within 30 days.

What happens

Account closed
Sign-in disabled, profile removed
Data erased
Or anonymized where noted below
Within 30 days
You'll get an email once it's done

What gets deleted vs. retained

Deleted immediately
Profile — username, display name, avatar
Ratings & progression — skill ratings, level, XP, coins, gems
Notifications & devices — notification history, push device tokens
Sign-in credentials — your Google account link is removed from Tweak; nothing changes on your Google account itself
Retained for a limited time
Match history you were part of — de-identified so it can no longer be linked back to you, but kept in aggregate for other players' match records and platform statistics
Open reports or disputes — if you filed or are named in an active moderation report, minimal records are kept until it's resolved

This list describes Tweak's account deletion process in general terms.

Playing as a guest?

Guest accounts aren't tied to an email, so we can't look one up from this page. Open Tweak, go to Settings → Delete Account while still signed in to that guest session — that removes it immediately. If you've already lost access to the session, contact us below with your device and the approximate date you played.
